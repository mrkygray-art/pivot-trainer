# SOP-104: Customer Footage Requests

Harborline Security Services (fictional). Applies to all field technicians and project managers.

## 1 Purpose

Recorded video can show people who did not agree to be shared. This SOP makes sure footage is only released to the right person, with the right approval, and with a record of every step.

## 2 Receiving a request

### 2.1 Who can ask

A footage request can come from a customer employee, a property manager, an insurance adjuster, or a member of the public. Treat every request the same way until the requestor is verified.

### 2.2 Log the request first

Before doing anything else, open a Footage Request ticket. Record the requestor's name, phone number, the site, the camera or area, and the date and time range they want.

### 2.3 Do not confirm what exists

Do not tell the requestor whether footage of the event exists until the request is approved. Say: "I have logged your request and the site's authorized contact will review it."

## 3 Verifying and approving

### 3.1 Verify the requestor

Call the requestor back using the phone number on the customer's site record, not the number they gave you. If they are not on the site record, the request must go to an authorized contact.

### 3.2 Get approval from an authorized contact

Only an authorized contact named on the site record can approve a release. Approval must be in writing, by email or in the ticket. Verbal approval is not enough.

### 3.3 Law enforcement requests

If police or another agency asks for footage, do not export anything yourself. Escalate the ticket to your project manager within 1 business day. The project manager coordinates with the customer.

### 3.4 Timeline

Most recorders overwrite video after 30 days. If approval has not arrived within 5 business days, the project manager contacts the authorized contact directly so footage is not lost.

## 4 Exporting footage

### 4.1 Export only the approved range

Export only the cameras and the time range in the written approval. If the requestor later asks for more, it is a new request and needs new approval.

### 4.2 Protect the file

Exports must be saved to an encrypted drive or the Harborline secure share. Never send footage as an email attachment or through a personal messaging app.

### 4.3 Name the file

Name each export with the ticket number, site code, camera, and date, for example `FR-2231_SITE14_CAM3_2026-03-02`.

## 5 Release and records

### 5.1 Custody log

Every person who touches the export signs the custody log in the ticket with their name, date, time, and what they did.

### 5.2 Release

Release the export only to the person named in the written approval. Record how and when it was delivered.

### 5.3 Closing the ticket

Close the ticket only after the custody log is complete and the delivery is recorded. Keep the ticket for 3 years.
